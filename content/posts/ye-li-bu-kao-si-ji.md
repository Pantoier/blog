<style>.poem .pl{line-height:1.9em;min-height:1.9em;margin:0;padding:0;font-size:1.1em;}</style>
<div class="poem" style="margin: 2em 0;">
<div class="pl">我穿着内裤站在阳台上</div>
<div class="pl">楼下走过一个死胖子</div>
<div class="pl">黑衣、黑裤</div>
<div class="pl">还挎着一个黑包</div>
<div class="pl">包里装着本红皮的诗集</div>
<div class="pl">两个月了</div>
<div class="pl">每天都背着这本书</div>
<div class="pl" style="font-weight:bold">真是个死胖子</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">又走来了一个女人</div>
<div class="pl">浅蓝色毛衣、白短裤、肉丝袜</div>
<div class="pl">可惜</div>
<div class="pl">是贫乳</div>
<div class="pl">她牵着一条腿短得可笑的狗</div>
<div class="pl">不对</div>
<div class="pl">是狗拉着她</div>
<div class="pl">那短腿的狗</div>
<div class="pl">正在枇杷树下</div>
<div class="pl">像跳芭蕾一样转圈</div>
<div class="pl">它弓起身子</div>
<div class="pl">放下屁股</div>
<div class="pl">不好</div>
<div class="pl">它看见了我</div>
<div class="pl">眼神里都是警惕</div>
<div class="pl" style="font-weight:bold">好像拉出来的是</div>
<div class="pl" style="font-weight:bold">能让我变聪明的哲学</div>
<div class="pl" style="font-weight:bold">不是屎</div>
<div class="pl">女人嫌弃地挥手</div>
<div class="pl">好一会儿</div>
<div class="pl">这便秘的狗才拉着她</div>
<div class="pl">一起扭着屁股走了</div>
<div class="pl">留下一堆屎</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">又走来一个年轻人</div>
<div class="pl">西装、领带、皮鞋</div>
<div class="pl">背着鼓鼓的书包</div>
<div class="pl">低头划着手机</div>
<div class="pl">他走得飞快</div>
<div class="pl">这是赶着去干什么？</div>
<div class="pl">干！</div>
<div class="pl">我甚至不想看见他</div>
<div class="pl">而我</div>
<div class="pl" style="font-weight:bold">可以不脱衣服</div>
<div class="pl" style="font-weight:bold">就上床</div>
</div>
