<style>.poem .pl{line-height:1.9em;min-height:1.9em;margin:0;padding:0;font-size:1.1em;}</style>

<div class="poem">
<div class="pl">洁白的米饭</div>
<div class="pl">像垦丁纯净的沙滩</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">翠绿的菠菜</div>
<div class="pl">像瑞丽晶透的翡翠</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">黄糯的土豆</div>
<div class="pl">像昆仑温润的黄玉</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">牛肉</div>
<div class="pl">啊，牛肉</div>
<div class="pl">虽然我不知道你像什么</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">但嚼着你</div>
<div class="pl">眼泪就止不住的落下</div>
<div class="pl">仿佛尝到了工地上的牛马</div>
<div class="pl">仿佛尝到了工厂中的牛马</div>
<div class="pl">仿佛尝到了办公室里的牛马</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">你的肉是那么的老</div>
<div class="pl">像那一副副布满沧桑又略显麻木的面庞</div>
<div class="pl">你的味是那么的淡</div>
<div class="pl">像那一双双干枯黝黑又透出虚弱的手掌</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">我的泪再也止不住</div>
<div class="pl">就像生活的苦果一样</div>
<div class="pl">就像我无法把你咽下</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">无法下咽</div>
<div class="pl">我只能不停地嚼</div>
<div class="pl">不停地嚼</div>
<div class="pl">左边嚼完</div>
<div class="pl">右边再嚼</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">因为不忍把你抛弃</div>
<div class="pl">因为不忍把你遗忘</div>
<div class="pl">我要将你狠狠地咽下</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">让我咽下你吧</div>
<div class="pl">再去迎接明天的辉煌</div>
</div>
