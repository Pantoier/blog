<div class="poem">
<div class="pl">广州</div>
<div class="pl">南国之都</div>
<div class="pl">在天上</div>
<div class="pl">等一阵雷雨过后</div>
<div class="pl">终于落了地</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">耳边又响起了</div>
<div class="pl">机场悦耳的粤语广播</div>
<div class="pl">跟歌一样</div>
<div class="pl">让人想哼哼</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">出了机场</div>
<div class="pl">雨后的天气</div>
<div class="pl">就像南京的梅雨天</div>
<div class="pl">潮潮的</div>
<div class="pl">湿湿的</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">深吸一口</div>
<div class="pl">掐灭手上的烟头</div>
<div class="pl">上车</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">车上放着</div>
<div class="pl">李克勤的《月半小夜曲》</div>
<div class="pl">人如天上的明月</div>
<div class="pl">是不可拥有</div>
</div>
