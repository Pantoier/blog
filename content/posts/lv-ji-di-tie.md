<div class="poem">
<div class="pl">青色的短发</div>
<div class="pl">银色的耳钉</div>
<div class="pl">淡淡的香味</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">你贴在门的那边</div>
<div class="pl">我贴在门的这边</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">你背着LV包包挤地铁</div>
<div class="pl">LV包包上印着许多的LV</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">我也背着包包挤地铁</div>
<div class="pl">不是LV</div>
<div class="pl">但包上贴着字</div>
<div class="pl">上面写着</div>
<div class="pl" style="font-weight:bold">别和儿子生气</div>
</div>
