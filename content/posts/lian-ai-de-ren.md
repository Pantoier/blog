<style>.poem .pl{line-height:1.9em;min-height:1.9em;margin:0;padding:0;font-size:1.1em;}</style>

<div class="poem">
<div class="pl">恋爱的人呀</div>
<div class="pl">从我身边走过</div>
<div class="pl">牵着手儿</div>
<div class="pl">幸福都挂在了脸上</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">恋爱的人呀</div>
<div class="pl">我从你们身边走过</div>
<div class="pl">相拥在河边</div>
<div class="pl">脸儿轻轻埋进怀里</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">恋爱的人呀</div>
<div class="pl">我低头快快地走</div>
<div class="pl">假装翻手机</div>
<div class="pl">却瞥见你眉间的欢喜</div>
<div class="pl"></div>
<div class="pl">恋爱的人呀</div>
<div class="pl">要珍惜这良辰美景</div>
<div class="pl">多好的月</div>
<div class="pl">让人想起昔年月下的她</div>
</div>
